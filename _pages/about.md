I am an Associate Professor of Mathematics at Colorado State University, and I am currently exploring a transition out of academia.

My background is in mathematics, computer science, and teaching. Much of my work has involved organizing abstract ideas, communicating difficult technical material clearly, and helping people reason through complex structures.

I am interested in roles where I can bring mathematical thinking, careful analysis, and clear communication to problems outside academia. I am especially drawn to work that sits between technical depth and decision-making, where understanding complex systems and explaining them well are important.
